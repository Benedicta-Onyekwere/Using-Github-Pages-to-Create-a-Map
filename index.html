Liberia: Crisis & Social Response

An integrated data project exploring the intersection of environmental hazards, public health infrastructure, and social indicators in Liberia. This project visualizes flood risks, identifies at-risk health facilities, and maps gender-based violence prevalence.

Datasets Used

Global Flood Archive

Source: HDX / OCHA
Format: GeoJSON / CSV
Retrieved: April 2026

Comprehensive record of global flood events since 1985, localized for Liberia analysis.

Health Facilities

Source: OpenStreetMap
Format: GeoJSON
Retrieved: April 2026

Location and metadata for clinics, hospitals, and pharmacies across all Liberian counties.

GBV Indicators

Source: LDHS (DHS Program)
Format: Enriched GeoJSON
Retrieved: April 2026

Sub-national indicators for gender-based violence and FGM prevalence in Grand Cape Mount.
